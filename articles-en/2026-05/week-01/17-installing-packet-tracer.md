## Summary

Installing Packet Tracer is the first practical step before building labs. The goal is simple: download it, install it, launch it and sign in so future lessons can focus on networking instead of setup.

Main idea: do not overcomplicate the installation. Get the tool working and move on to learning.

## Key Points

- Packet Tracer is used as an early hands-on lab environment.
- Cisco typically provides it through Skills for All / NetAcad access.
- A free account may be required.
- Choose the installer for the correct operating system.
- Install with default options unless there is a clear reason not to.
- First launch may require sign-in.
- The goal is not to master the tool yet.
- The win is simply opening Packet Tracer successfully.

## Notes

### Basic Flow

```text
Find Packet Tracer
Create/sign in to Cisco account
Enroll if required
Download installer
Install
Launch
Sign in
```

### Keep It Simple

Do not spend the first lesson optimizing every setting. If the app installs cleanly and launches, the environment is ready.

Momentum matters at the beginning.
