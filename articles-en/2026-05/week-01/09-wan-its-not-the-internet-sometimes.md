## Summary

WAN means Wide Area Network. In everyday conversation, people often use WAN to mean "the internet connection," but technically a WAN is any network that spans a large distance and connects separate locations.

Main idea: the internet is a WAN, but not every WAN is simply "the internet."

## Key Points

- LAN means Local Area Network.
- WAN means Wide Area Network.
- LAN covers a local area like a home, office or coffee shop.
- WAN connects across larger distances.
- The internet is the most common WAN people interact with.
- Companies can also have private WAN links between sites.
- WAN connectivity may use ISP circuits, fiber, VPNs or other services.
- WAN design affects reliability, performance and cost.

## Notes

### LAN vs WAN

Simple model:

```text
LAN = inside the location
WAN = beyond the location
```

At NetworkChuck Coffee, devices inside the shop communicate on the LAN. When the shop reaches a cloud payment processor or another location, it uses WAN connectivity.

### Not Always Just Internet

WAN can mean:

- internet connection;
- site-to-site VPN;
- private circuit;
- MPLS-style service;
- carrier Ethernet;
- connection between branches.

### Why It Matters

If a business depends on multiple locations, WAN design becomes part of business continuity.

## Questions

### Is WAN always the internet?

No. The internet is a WAN, but private or provider-managed WANs also exist.

### What is the basic LAN/WAN distinction?

LAN is local. WAN connects beyond the local area.
