# Present Simple

## Когда используется

1. Регулярные действия и привычки.
2. Факты и общеизвестная информация.
3. Расписания и графики.
4. Состояния, чувства, мнения.

## Формула образования

### Positive Sentences

| Subject | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|
| I / We / You / They | V1 | something |
| He / She / It | V1 + `s/es` | something |

### Negative Sentences

| Subject | Auxiliary verb | Negative | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I / We / You / They | `do` | `not` / `don't` | V1 | something |
| He / She / It | `does` | `not` / `doesn't` | V1 | something |

### Question Sentences

| Question word | Auxiliary verb | Subject | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What / Why / Where / When / How | `do` | I / we / you / they | V1 | something? |
| What / Why / Where / When / How | `does` | he / she / it | V1 | something? |

## Маркеры времени

- `always`
- `usually`
- `often`
- `sometimes`
- `never`
- `every day`
- `on Mondays`

## Примеры предложений

1. I drink coffee every morning.
2. She works in a hospital.
3. We live near the river.
4. They play tennis on Sundays.
5. He likes classical music.
6. I usually wake up at seven.
7. You speak English very well.
8. My father drives to work.
9. We study grammar every week.
10. She watches the news every evening.
11. They often visit their grandparents.
12. I need more time.
13. He knows the answer.
14. The train leaves at 9 a.m.
15. We sometimes eat out.
16. She teaches mathematics.
17. I love this song.
18. They work hard.
19. The sun rises in the east.
20. You always help me.

## Вопросительные предложения

1. Do you drink coffee every morning?
2. Does she work in a hospital?
3. Do they play tennis on Sundays?
4. Does he like classical music?
5. Do you usually wake up at seven?
6. Does your father drive to work?
7. Do we study grammar every week?
8. Does she watch the news every evening?
9. Do they often visit their grandparents?
10. Does the train leave at 9 a.m.?

## Полезная заметка

После `does` основной глагол всегда идет в `V1`:

- `She works every day.`
- `Does she work every day?`
