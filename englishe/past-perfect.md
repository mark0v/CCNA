# Past Perfect

## Когда используется

1. Одно действие произошло раньше другого в прошлом.
2. Нужно показать, что к определенному моменту в прошлом действие уже завершилось.
3. Часто используется в рассказах и последовательностях событий.

## Формула образования

### Positive & Negative Sentences

| Subject | Auxiliary verb | Negative | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I / We / You / They / He / She / It | `had` | `not` / `hadn't` | V3 | something |

### Question Sentences

| Question word | Auxiliary verb | Subject | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What / Why / Where / When / Who | `had` | I / we / you / they / he / she / it | V3 | something? |

## Маркеры времени

- `before`
- `after`
- `by the time`
- `already`
- `just`
- `never`

## Примеры предложений

1. I had finished my work before dinner.
2. She had left when I arrived.
3. We had already eaten before the guests came.
4. They had never seen the sea before that trip.
5. He had broken his phone before he bought a new one.
6. I had cleaned the room before my parents came home.
7. You had told me the truth earlier.
8. My brother had gone to bed before midnight.
9. We had studied the topic before the test.
10. She had written the letter by the time I called.
11. They had sold the car before moving abroad.
12. I had met him once before.
13. He had forgotten my name.
14. The train had left before we reached the station.
15. We had prepared everything in advance.
16. She had never tried Indian food before.
17. I had saved enough money by last summer.
18. They had built the house by 2018.
19. The teacher had explained the rule before the exercise started.
20. You had made the right decision.

## Вопросительные предложения

1. Had you finished your work before dinner?
2. Had she left before you arrived?
3. Had they already eaten?
4. Had he broken his phone before buying a new one?
5. Had you told me the truth earlier?
6. Had your brother gone to bed before midnight?
7. Had we studied the topic before the test?
8. Had she written the letter before you called?
9. Had they sold the car before moving abroad?
10. Had the train left before you reached the station?

## Полезная заметка

`Past Perfect` часто нужен, когда в предложении два прошлых действия:

- более раннее действие: `had + V3`
- более позднее действие: `Past Simple`
