# Future Simple

## Когда используется

1. Спонтанное решение.
2. Предсказание или мнение о будущем.
3. Обещание, предложение, просьба.
4. Действие в будущем без точного плана.

## Формула образования

### Positive & Negative Sentences

| Subject | Auxiliary verb | Negative | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I / We / You / They / He / She / It | `will` | `not` / `won't` | V1 | something |

### Question Sentences

| Question word | Auxiliary verb | Subject | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What / Why / Where / When / Who | `will` | I / we / you / they / he / she / it | V1 | something? |

## Маркеры времени

- `tomorrow`
- `next week`
- `next year`
- `soon`
- `in the future`
- `I think`
- `probably`

## Примеры предложений

1. I will call you tomorrow.
2. She will help us with the project.
3. We will visit our friends next weekend.
4. They will arrive soon.
5. He will be happy to see you.
6. I will send the message later.
7. You will understand this rule soon.
8. My parents will come back on Monday.
9. We will need more time.
10. She will probably win the competition.
11. They will build a new school here.
12. I will remember your advice.
13. He will not forget this day.
14. The weather will change tomorrow.
15. We will meet after work.
16. She will answer your question.
17. I think it will rain tonight.
18. They will support you.
19. The teacher will explain everything.
20. You will do it well.

## Вопросительные предложения

1. Will you call me tomorrow?
2. Will she help us with the project?
3. Will they arrive soon?
4. Will he be happy to see me?
5. Will you send the message later?
6. Will your parents come back on Monday?
7. Will we need more time?
8. Will she answer my question?
9. Will it rain tonight?
10. Will the teacher explain everything?

## Полезная заметка

`Will` часто используется для решений в момент речи:

- `The phone is ringing. I will answer it.`
