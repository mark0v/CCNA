# Present Continuous

## Когда используется

1. Действие происходит прямо сейчас.
2. Временное действие или ситуация.
3. Запланированное действие в ближайшем будущем.
4. Раздражающая повторяющаяся ситуация с `always`.

## Формула образования

### Positive & Negative Sentences

| Subject | Auxiliary verb | Negative | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | `am` | `not` / `am not` | `V-ing` | something |
| He / She / It | `is` | `not` / `isn't` | `V-ing` | something |
| We / You / They | `are` | `not` / `aren't` | `V-ing` | something |

### Question Sentences

| Question word | Auxiliary verb | Subject | Main verb | Rest of sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What / Why / Where / Who | `am` | I | `V-ing` | something? |
| What / Why / Where / Who | `is` | he / she / it | `V-ing` | something? |
| What / Why / Where / Who | `are` | we / you / they | `V-ing` | something? |

## Маркеры времени

- `now`
- `right now`
- `at the moment`
- `today`
- `currently`

2. She is talking to her friend.
3. We are having lunch at the moment.
4. They are playing basketball outside.
5. He is working from home today.
6. I am looking for my phone.
7. You are doing very well.
8. My sister is preparing dinner.
9. We are staying with our cousins this week.
10. She is wearing a red jacket.
11. They are studying for the exam.
12. I am writing an email now.
13. He is waiting for the doctor.
14. The children are sleeping.
15. It is getting dark.
16. We are planning a trip for next month.
17. She is always losing her keys.
18. I am learning new words.
19. They are building a new bridge.
20. You are smiling.

## Вопросительные предложения

10. Is it getting dark?

## Полезная заметка

`Present Continuous` показывает процесс, а `Present Simple` — обычное действие:

- `She works every day.`
- `She is working now.`
